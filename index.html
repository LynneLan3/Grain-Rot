What is Grain Rot?

Grain Rot is a horror co-op extraction builder set in a scorched wasteland. You play as a Living Spark inside fragile wooden vessels, descend into shifting underground ruins with your crew, scavenge furniture and loot, then try to extract before the Corrupted close in. Everything you haul back rebuilds your Outpost for the next run.

The game is developed by indie duo Beck & Branch Games and published by Neem Interactive. Official materials describe it as co-op extraction horror with physics-driven chaos, Outpost progression, and procedurally shifting Deep Layers.

Gameplay Overview

Based on the official Steam store page and launch materials, core Grain Rot game features include:

  • Possess and swap between fragile wooden vessels as a Living Spark
  • Co-op extraction runs into procedurally shifting underground dungeons
  • Smash furniture for resources or feed debris into the Grinder for gold
  • Persistent Outpost rebuilding with interactive furniture and upgrades
  • Corrupted vessels that react to sound, movement, and each other
  • When your vessel breaks, stay in the run as a Spark and search for another host

Currently, no official announcement confirms a full boss list, ending count, or complete character roster beyond the Sanctuary Outpost brothers Biggie and Murch named in launch press materials.
