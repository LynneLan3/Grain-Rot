## About GRAIN ROT

**GRAIN ROT** is a co-op extraction-horror and builder game from Beck & Branch Games, published by Neem. The official Steam listing describes runs into shifting underground ruins, scavenging and extraction, and rebuilding an Outpost between runs.

This hub keeps the public information limited to facts recorded in the site's internal research evidence and the cited official sources. See the Guides above for the confirmed launch, platform, PC requirement, and online co-op details.
