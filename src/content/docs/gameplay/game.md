## GRAIN ROT gameplay overview

GRAIN ROT is described in the official Steam listing as a co-op extraction-horror and builder game. Players descend into shifting underground ruins, search for loot, and try to extract what they found before returning to rebuild their Outpost.

The game uses fragile wooden vessels and Living Sparks as part of its premise. Launch materials also describe persistent Outpost rebuilding and physics-driven chaos. These are the confirmed high-level systems currently supported by the available sources; this page does not fill in an unverified progression or item database.

## What is confirmed about a run?

- The setting includes shifting underground ruins and Deep Layers.
- The objective includes scavenging and extracting loot.
- The Outpost can be rebuilt between runs.
- The game supports online co-op, with the available launch evidence listing 1–4 players.

For the player-count details and unsupported multiplayer assumptions, see the [GRAIN ROT multiplayer guide](/grain-rot/multiplayer/).

## What this site does not claim

The current evidence does not establish a complete list of characters, bosses, maps, quests, builds, or crafting recipes. Those topics remain outside this guide until a reliable source supports them.
