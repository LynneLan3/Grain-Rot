## What is GRAIN ROT?

GRAIN ROT is a co-op extraction-horror and builder game where Living Sparks descend into shifting underground Deep Layers inside fragile wooden vessels, scavenge loot, escape, and use what they recover to rebuild a persistent Outpost.

Each run is about entering the underground ruins, searching for loot and furniture, surviving Corrupted threats and environmental risk, then extracting successfully so the haul can fuel Outpost progression. Wooden vessels are fragile; when one breaks, the Living Spark can keep going and look for another host. The game supports solo play and online co-op.

## How does a GRAIN ROT run work?

The confirmed high-level loop looks like this:

1. Prepare at the Outpost, then begin a descent into the underground ruins.
2. Ride the cursed elevator into the shifting Deep Layers with your crew (or alone).
3. Search for loot, smash or collect furniture, and gather other recoverable resources.
4. Face Corrupted threats and the rising risk of going deeper.
5. Extract successfully with what you hauled—or risk losing unsecured loot.
6. Return to the Outpost with what you brought back.
7. Use recovered resources and furniture to rebuild, unlock upgrades, and strengthen the hub.
8. Descend again, aiming for deeper layers as the Outpost improves.

Exact run length, fixed floor counts, and extraction timers are not treated as confirmed facts on this page.

## What are Living Sparks and wooden vessels?

You play as a **Living Spark** surviving inside a **wooden vessel**. Official materials describe those vessels as fragile: they can splinter, collapse, and catch fire.

That vessel-as-body setup is one of the clearest ways GRAIN ROT differs from a typical extraction shooter. You are not only managing inventory and escape routes—you are also managing a body that can fail mid-run. The Steam listing also highlights possessing and swapping between fragile wooden vessels as a core feature.

## What happens when your vessel breaks?

When a vessel breaks, the Living Spark escapes. Death is framed as a change of scenery rather than an instant full wipe for that player.

Official description says you stay in the run as a Spark: you can guide your team through the dark, search for another host (with a new set of abilities), and try to get back into a vessel before you fade out completely. If everyone breaks, the loot from that run is lost.

This page does not claim exact fade timers, invulnerability windows, revive rules, or teammate-carry mechanics beyond what the listed sources support.

## How loot and extraction work

One of the main goals in the Deep Layers is scavenging valuable finds—loot and furniture you can rip out or smash for resources.

Successful extraction matters because only what you haul back feeds Outpost rebuilding. If the run collapses and everyone breaks, that run’s loot is lost. Furniture is not just decoration: it ties into resource gathering, the Grinder, and later Outpost progression.

This guide does not publish item values, rarity tiers, best loot routes, or farming meta.

## What is the Grinder?

The **Grinder** is part of the resource loop described in the official Steam materials. You can smash furniture for resources, or feed finds into the Grinder for gold.

That creates a practical choice: keep or use an item for rebuilding and interaction, or convert it through the Grinder for gold value. This page does not invent conversion ratios, gold tables, or “best items to grind.”

## What does the Outpost do?

The **Outpost** is the persistent hub between runs. Everything you successfully haul back helps rebuild it.

Confirmed progression direction from the official listing includes interactive furniture unlocks, stat upgrades, rescued survivors, and other ways to improve survival on the next descent. As the Outpost gets stronger, you can afford to go deeper.

Exact upgrade trees, building costs, NPC unlock order, and numbered stat tables are outside the current evidence boundary.

## What are the Deep Layers?

The **Deep Layers** are the shifting underground ruins where extraction runs happen. Official materials describe procedurally shifting underground dungeons and a cursed elevator ride into those ruins with your crew.

The deeper you go, the worse the Rot becomes. Environment and layout are not presented as a single fixed tourist map—they shift, and risk rises with depth.

## What are the Corrupted?

The **Corrupted** are hostile threats in the Deep Layers. Official copy describes Corrupted vessels that react to sound, movement, and each other in unpredictable ways.

This is a high-level threat overview only. Enemy HP, damage numbers, weaknesses, spawn tables, and boss lists are not confirmed here.

## Is GRAIN ROT solo or co-op?

GRAIN ROT supports single-player and online co-op. Available launch evidence used by this site lists a **1–4 player** range for online co-op. Co-op is a core selling point—physics-driven chaos and crew descents are central to the pitch—but solo play is supported.

Outpost rebuilding and persistent progression are part of the confirmed design. Successful extraction is what turns a dangerous run into long-term growth: haul resources back, rebuild the hub, unlock improvements, then push deeper.
