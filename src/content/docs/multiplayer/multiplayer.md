## Does GRAIN ROT have online co-op?

Yes. The available Steam and launch evidence supports **online co-op**, with launch materials listing a **1–4 player** range.

## What is not confirmed?

The research evidence does not confirm cross-play, local co-op, or a competitive multiplayer mode. This guide therefore limits its claim to the documented online co-op feature and player range.

For the broader run structure, see [what GRAIN ROT is and how its gameplay loop is described](/grain-rot/game/).
