Grain Rot Game Multiplayer

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Official launch materials for the Grain Rot game list 1–4 online co-op. That is the confirmed player range from Beck & Branch Games and Neem Interactive press facts published with the August 7, 2026 Steam launch.

Player limit

Mode Online co-op
Players 1–4
Maximum lobby size 4 players (per official press facts)
Source Games Press launch fact sheet / Neem Interactive

If you are searching “Grain Rot how many players” or “Grain Rot player limit,” the official answer today is up to four players in online co-op.

Solo play

Because the stated range is 1–4, solo play is included. Official materials frame the fantasy around a crew, but they do not require four players to start.

Online features

The Steam store page for the Grain Rot game notes interactive elements including in-game chat and online interactivity. Co-op extraction, physics-driven chaos, and Outpost progression are described as shared crew activities in official copy.

Steam system requirements also list a broadband Internet connection for both minimum and recommended specs.

Not officially detailed

Currently, no official announcement confirms cross-play with consoles (none are announced), dedicated servers vs. peer hosting details, ranked modes, or a separate competitive multiplayer mode beyond the stated 1–4 online co-op extraction experience.
